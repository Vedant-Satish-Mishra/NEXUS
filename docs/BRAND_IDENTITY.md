# NEXUS brand identity

## Confirmed foundation

NEXUS is described as a local-first, multi-agent cognitive system. The supplied visual references use a node-like orange N mark, dark cocoa, warm cream, burnt orange, and imperfect hand-drawn contours. The proposed expansion is “Networked Executive for eXtensible Understanding & Self-learning.”

## Proposed positioning

NEXUS helps a user see how a local AI response is planned, grounded, checked, and controlled. Its differentiator is transparent coordination rather than a claim of superior intelligence or AGI.

## Personality and voice

- Calm, capable, transparent, and study-oriented.
- Explain what is happening without pretending certainty.
- Use “your plan”, “your sources”, and “waiting for your approval” rather than autonomous or magical language.
- Never imply that data leaves the device unless an opted-in external tool is actually in use.

## Visual attributes

Warm technical, tangible, and deliberate—not futuristic neon, sterile enterprise blue, or playful mascot-driven. The hand-drawn contour is a recognisable frame, not an effect on every element.

## Messaging rules

Say “local-first”, “source-grounded”, “you approve plans”, and “confirmed memories”. Avoid “AGI”, “always accurate”, “thinks like a human”, fabricated performance claims, and invented customer stories.
