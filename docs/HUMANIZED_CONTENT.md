# NEXUS content style

Write naturally, plainly, and with evidence. Use active voice and short explanations. Do not use generic AI hype, fake testimonials, invented statistics, or “revolutionary” claims.

## Voice patterns

- Heading: “Plan ready — your call” not “AI orchestration complete”.
- CTA: “Start chat”, “Approve plan”, “Upload files”, “Save memory”.
- Help: “Nothing runs until you approve this plan.”
- Error: “We couldn’t reach the local model. Check that Ollama is running, then retry.”
- Empty state: “No documents yet. Upload a file to use it as a source.”

## Before / after

- Avoid: “Our advanced AI will intelligently revolutionise your workflow.”
- Prefer: “NEXUS can plan the task, search your sources, and show you what it used.”

Avoid promising that answers are correct. Say “I don’t have enough evidence in your sources to answer that” when appropriate.
